Reporting year 2027 · Climate disclosure evidence intake

Upload your evidence and verification starts right away

Each site uploads its electricity and fuel evidence; the HQ ESG team uploads the investment contracts and the HQ calculation sheet. The system bundles them per entity, verifies them, and sends the outcome as a verification report.

Submit Evidence

The three files are merged into a single bundle for the entity before verification.

What happens next

  1. Intake The files from the two sites and HQ are merged into one bundle for the entity, and a receipt no. is issued.
  2. Verification Electricity use, Scope 1 and 2, and climate-related investment are recalculated from the evidence and compared with the HQ calculation sheet.
  3. Result The bundle is rated Ready to Disclose, Needs Correction, or Needs Review, and a verification report is sent.

Documents to include

  • Electricity usage certificate or electricity bill for each site
  • City gas bill showing annual usage, diesel tax invoices
  • Climate-related investment contracts
  • HQ calculation sheet

Common reasons for return

  • Blurry scans with unreadable figures
  • The same tax invoice included twice (duplicate)
  • Fuel evidence missing for one site
This is an intake desk for a hackathon demo. Do not upload real company documents or files containing personal information.
